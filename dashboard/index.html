What do these states mean?
🟢 IN TRADE
Holding a position.
⚡ ENTERING
The entry condition fired on this candle and passed every filter.
🚫 BLOCKED
The entry condition fired and a filter rejected it. The filter that rejected it is named in the row.
➖ NO SETUP
RSI has not crossed up through oversold, so there is nothing to act on. Nothing is wrong — this is the normal resting state and what you should expect to see most of the time.
🌙 MARKET CLOSED
Stock bot outside market hours. Not evaluating at all.

States describe what the bot is doing, not how likely a trade is.
